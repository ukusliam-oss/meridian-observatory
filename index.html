Syllabus changes

What has changedfor 2027

Economics and Geography have revised syllabuses for exams from 2027, with changes to the papers. History has been updated, but the content and papers are mostly the same. Check that your revision materials match the syllabus you are taking.

History 0470

Minor update
  • Content and papersNo significant changes to teaching for 2027 and 2028.
  • Paper 2, June 2027 (Option B)How secure was the USSR's control over Eastern Europe, 1948–c.1989?
  • Paper 2, November 2027 (Option B)Was the Treaty of Versailles fair?
  • Paper 41 hour, 40 marks. One question in two parts: (a) an account and (b) importance.

The Paper 2 topics for each exam series are listed on the History page.

Economics 0455

Revised
  • Paper 130 questions, 45 minutes → 40 questions, 1 hour
  • Paper 290 marks, 2 hours 15 minutes → 80 marks, 2 hours
  • Paper 2 Section A30 marks → 20 marks, parts (a) to (f)
  • Assessment objectivesThe weighting of AO3 Evaluation has decreased to 10%. AO1 and AO2 have increased.
  • New contentIncludes changes in globalisation, recession, national minimum wage diagrams, ATC diagrams and types of merger.

New content is labelled topic by topic on the Economics page.

Geography 0460

Revised
  • Structure3 themes → 10 topics: 5 physical and 5 human
  • Paper 1Geographical Themes → Physical Geography, 1 hour 45 minutes, 75 marks
  • Paper 2Geographical Skills, 1 hour 30 minutes, 60 marks → Human Geography, 1 hour 45 minutes, 75 marks
  • TopicsClimate change is a new topic. Settlement, water and weather have been removed.
  • TerminologyMEDC and LEDC → HIC, MIC and LIC
  • Examplescase studies → 16 detailed specific examples

All 16 detailed specific examples are listed on the Geography page.

Exam technique

Commandwords

The command word tells you what kind of answer to give. In all three subjects, questions range from recalling knowledge to making a judgement. Select a level to see example questions.

Daily quiz

Today'ssix questions

Two questions from each subject. Everyone gets the same six questions today, so you can compare scores with your classmates. After that, each set is chosen at random.
