A named eruption

Iceland, April 2010Eyjafjallajökull

Scroll

Eyjafjallajökull is a strato-volcano covered by an ice cap, shown here with its heights raised one and a half times.

The summit eruption began on 14 April 2010 under the ice. Fine ash spread over Europe and closed most airspace from 15 to 20 April.

Meltwater floods poured down the valleys, and about 800 local people were evacuated.

Move across the ice to see which way its meltwater runs: the steepest way down, calculated from the heights.
