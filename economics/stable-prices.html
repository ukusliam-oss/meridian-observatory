Inflation was low almost everywhere in 2019.

By 2022 it had risen sharply in most countries.

Then it fell again by 2024.

Many governments give their central bank a target. The US Federal Reserve has aimed for 2% since 2012.

The warm line marks 2%: a country is above it where the line runs round its side. Point at a country to read its inflation.
